The Space

Made for the long stay.

Driftwood is designed around warm caramel tones, natural wood, soft linen, and sculptural driftwood accents. It feels considered without feeling precious, the kind of place where you unpack properly on day one because you already know you will be back from the beach by five and cooking by six.

The king bed is the centerpiece, and the dedicated workspace is a real desk, not an afterthought, so a working week here feels like a working week, just with better weather. The private balcony is for morning coffee and evening wind-downs. Step outside and you are a short walk from Downtown Hollywood's restaurants, cocktail bars, and live music.

Staying longer? Weekly and monthly discounts apply automatically, and mid-stay cleanings can be added anytime. Driftwood is set up for weeks, not just weekends.
