The Space

Light, calm, and ready when you are.

Coastal Blue is the kind of place that slows you down in the right way. Mornings start on the balcony with coffee. Afternoons are for the beach, the Boardwalk, or nowhere in particular. Evenings are for Hollywood Blvd, two minutes away on foot, where dinner and live music are waiting.

Inside, everything is set up for a real stay, not a weekend crash pad. Cook a full dinner in the kitchen. Get actual work done at a real desk with high-speed Wi-Fi. Throw a load in the washer and dryer. Fall asleep in a queen bed and wake up to a place that was professionally cleaned and inspected before you arrived.

Coming for a cruise or flying in late? The unit is set up for self check-in, and we send your access details and a digital guidebook before arrival, so you walk in like you live here.
