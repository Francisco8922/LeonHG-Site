The Space

Soft neutrals, real workspace, a balcony with a view.

Stone and Steel is the calm one. Warm taupe walls, natural wood, and a gray sofa that invites an afternoon with a book. The balcony is not a token slab, it has chairs, a table, and a real view over the neighborhood. Mornings start at the coffee bar. Evenings end on the balcony, or two minutes away on Hollywood Blvd.

Inside, everything is set up for a real stay, not a weekend crash pad, and for work that actually gets done. There is a dedicated desk with high-speed Wi-Fi, a full kitchen with an island made for lingering, and a washer and dryer. Stream on the Smart TV. Fall asleep in a real bed and wake up to a place that was professionally cleaned and inspected before you arrived.

Coming for a cruise or flying in late? The unit is set up for self check-in, and we send your access details and a digital guidebook before arrival, so you walk in like you live here.
