Good to Know Before You Book

Check-in & check-out

Check-in is 4:00 PM and check-out is 10:00 AM at both units. Self check-in with a lockbox, and we send your access details and a digital guidebook before arrival.

Parking

One parking hang tag per unit, first come, first served in the building garage. Display the tag on your rearview mirror.

Getting around the building

The elevator is out of service until further notice, so please plan on stairs to the 3rd floor. We will update this page the day it returns.

House rules, simply

Maximum 4 guests. One dog up to 35 lbs is welcome ($30/day). No smoking in the unit or on the balcony, no parties, and quiet hours are 11:00 PM to 7:00 AM.

A note on the rooftop: the building's rooftop pool is not open at this time, so we do not list it as an amenity. If that changes, this page will say so.
